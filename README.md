# Gabriel Marcel — Open Digital Corpus

An open-access, bilingual digital reader for the philosophical and dramatic works of Gabriel Marcel (1889–1973).

## Features
- Aligned French source text with philosophical English translation.
- Interactive Marcelian philosophical glossary (enforcing terms like *disponibilité*, *l'exigence ontologique*, and *le monde cassé*).
- Synchronized paragraph tracking and reading mode toggles.
- Companion work cross-referencing between plays and philosophical essays.

## Public Domain Notice
Gabriel Marcel's original French writings entered the public domain in France and the European Union on January 1, 2024 (70 years post-mortem).
